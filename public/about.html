📋 E-Filing System
Digital Filing

Seamless 4-Step E-Filing Wizard

Our guided complaint wizard walks every citizen through a structured, clear submission. No legal jargon, no confusing forms — just simple steps to formal justice.

  • Select complaint type and court jurisdiction
  • Enter party details — complainant and respondent
  • Write a detailed description of the dispute
  • Attach evidence documents, photos, or PDFs (up to 10 MB)
  • Submit and instantly receive a reference number
⚖️ Real-Time Tracking
Case Lifecycle

Real-Time Case Status Tracking

Once filed, every complaint has a full, live status trail — both the complainant and court staff can monitor every stage instantly, without needing to call anyone.

  • Pending — Awaiting clerk review
  • In Progress — Active investigation or hearings
  • Under Review — Judge has the matter
  • Scheduled — Hearing date confirmed
  • Resolved / Rejected — Final disposition recorded
🛠️ Staff Console
Multi-Role Platform

A Full Staff Collaboration Environment

Justice Connect is not just a citizen portal — it's a complete judicial workspace. Each role gets tailored dashboards, controls, and permissions with no information leakage.

  • Admins access global analytics and system oversight
  • Clerks manage registry queues and serve notices
  • Judges issue orders, rulings, and schedule hearings
  • Respondents file formal responses and counter-claims
  • CSV exports for audit compliance
🎥 Virtual Hearings
Modern Justice

Virtual Hearings via Jitsi Meet

When physical presence isn't possible, Justice Connect provides integrated virtual hearing rooms powered by Jitsi Meet — accessible from any device, securely within the platform.

  • Judges chair virtual hearings from the dashboard
  • Respondents can join from their portal
  • Secure, unique room per case — no overlap risk
  • No external app required — runs in the browser
User Roles

Who Uses Justice Connect?

The platform serves five distinct user roles, each with tailored dashboards and access rights:

🧑

Citizen

File new complaints, track status, upload evidence, and communicate via the live remarks timeline.

📝

Registry Clerk

Review incoming filings, validate submissions, assign to judges, and send official court notices.

⚖️

Judge

Access assigned caseloads, schedule hearings, issue orders, post rulings, and write confidential notes.

🛠️

Administrator

Full system oversight — analytics, user management, export reports, and complaint deletion.

🧑‍⚖️

Respondent

View served cases, read judge/clerk remarks, file formal responses, and submit counter-claims.
